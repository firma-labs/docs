The shape of the thing — who holds the mission, who coordinates the work, and who builds, deploys, powers, and proves. And, plainly, how a decision actually gets made.

## The Shape

Two kinds of authority under one House — and the entities that do the building.

Firma is an **Order** — not a company, not a corporation. One collective holding many fields of stewardship, governed under covenant. At the top is the **Nation of Heaven** — the vision and the twelve Districts it builds toward. Under it is the **House of Saints**, the sacred governing body — and the House is two bodies together: the **Divine Network Assembly (DNA)** and **EDEN**. The DNA holds spiritual and constitutional authority: a set of **spiritual councils that cover but do not govern**, and a **Vision Council** of seven Vision Stewards — the **Firma Sovereign Foundation** — which is the only voting body. **EDEN** is the ministry council that coordinates execution. The operating entities — the ones writing code, pouring concrete, and moving capital — are **governed by the House but legally separate from it**. Each receives its mandate from above and operates freely within it.

## Two Kinds of Authority

Spiritual and legal authority are deliberately kept separate.

This is one of the most important things to understand early. **The spiritual covers and guides; the constitutional governs; execution runs the work.** Inside the DNA, the **spiritual councils** — Elder, Revelatory, Prayer, Wives — offer wisdom, intercession, and covenant accountability, but they do not govern. The **Vision Council** (the seven Vision Stewards, the Firma Sovereign Foundation) holds constitutional authority and is the only body that votes. **EDEN** coordinates execution and does not vote. When a steward faces a moral or covenant question beyond operational judgment, the covering speaks into it — it advises; it does not decide the operation. The **DNA Protocol** is the coded extension of all this: a derivative of what the DNA councils establish, it **locks execution and operating into the agreed-upon direction** — guardrails and real-time attestation that run inside the work itself, not bolted on after.

### The covering does not run the company. The company does not govern the covenant.

Each protects the other. Spiritual authority keeps the mission honest; legal authority keeps the work moving. Neither absorbs the other's job.

## Three Tiers

Authority flows down through three tiers — and only the top one votes.

- Tier 1**Constitutional Authority — the Vision Council.** The seven Vision Stewards — the **Firma Sovereign Foundation** — are the board and the **only voting body in the entire system**. They hold the Canon, govern Firmamint, authorize FIG, sign sovereign partnerships, and seat new Stewards. Alongside them in the DNA, the spiritual councils cover but do not govern. The Canon governs everything, including the stewards themselves.
- Tier 2**Execution Coordination — EDEN.** A coordination table, not a parliament. The Chair decides at the EDEN level; ministers report on their domains; when lanes intersect, EDEN synchronizes the work. **EDEN does not vote** — it directs and coordinates.
- Tier 3**Operating Entities.** Firma Labs, Edge, Photon, and Research. They build, deploy, power, and prove — governed by the House but legally separate. Each receives its mandate from above and operates freely within it.

The Stewards vote only on the few things that change the mission itself: a **new Vision Program** (5-of-7), a **Canon amendment** (unanimous 7-of-7), steward appointments/removals, FIG issuance, and sovereign partnerships. They do **not** vote on budgets, hiring, vendors, product decisions, or deployment timelines — all of that is execution, and it lives at EDEN and below.

## The Operating Entities

Four operating entities — and the hands and feet that reach both edges.

#### Firma Labs

The engineering entity — the protocol, the agentic OS, the substrate everything downstream runs on. FIT — the Order's ecosystem hands and feet — operates from here.

#### Firma Edge

The infrastructure entity — modular containers, mesh nodes, housing, compute hardware. Four divisions: Realm, SeedBase, Haven, Compute.

#### Firma Photon

The energy entity and sibling to Edge — solar and salt-ion batteries powering every deployment.

#### Firma Research

The research entity — invents and proves the frontier tech (orbital compute, post-quantum, space-solar), then graduates it into Edge to deploy.

The work needs carriers to both edges of the mission — one team to the frontier, one to the reach.

#### FIT — Firma Impact Team

The global ecosystem and field-activation team — ambassadors of Firma first, technical evangelists for Firmamint second. Operated by Labs, chartered by the Foundation to represent the whole Order.

#### CIK

Christ Is King — the ministry network within **Firma Theosis**. It accompanies the communities a deployment serves, so the rails serve the people who ride them, not just reach them.

The verbs are load-bearing. The Foundation **governs** (it doesn't fund or operate); EDEN **directs**; Edge **deploys**; CIK **accompanies**. Using the right verb for the right entity keeps the structure legible — see the Glossary for the full set.

## Agentic-Native

Building here is a different job — you orchestrate agent crews, not just do tasks.

This is the part that surprises people. Firma is **agentic-native and agentic-first**: autonomous agents — not humans clicking through tools — are the primary actors doing the work, and the work itself writes the record. That changes what your job is. You are not a worker assigned tasks; you are a **builder orchestrating crews of agents**, working in **queried, real-time generative environments** — you ask the system and it generates the state, the answer, the action on demand, instead of hunting through static dashboards.

**The split is clean:** humans carry judgment, relationships, and presence; agents carry execution, monitoring, and delivery. Governance lives *in* the work — the DNA Protocol's guardrails and real-time attestation run inside every action, not as a review afterward.

#### From doing → orchestrating

You direct crews of agents and judge their output. Your leverage is judgment and taste, not how many tasks you personally close.

#### From dashboards → queried & generative

You ask the environment and it generates the answer in real time. The system is alive and legible, not a stack of static reports.

#### From tall teams → flat & small

One builder plus agent crews produces what used to take a department, so the org stays **flatter and often smaller** — sixty people operating like six hundred.

This is why a Firma team can feel small and move large. We hire for the human things — judgment, care, presence — because the agents carry the volume, and we keep the structure flat so judgment travels fast.

## How a Decision Gets Made

Most decisions never go up. Here's the actual flow.

- 99%**The minister executes autonomously.** The vast majority of decisions happen inside a single domain, in real time. A DNA Protocol agent monitors for covenant drift; nobody waits for a vote.
- Deals**Two-key authorization.** Capital deployment takes two keys (Investment + Finance) — no committee vote.
- Cross-lane**EDEN convenes.** When two domains intersect, EDEN synchronizes and the Chair decides at the table.
- Mission**The Vision Stewards vote.** Only a new Vision Program (5-of-7) or a Canon amendment (unanimous 7-of-7) rises to the one voting body — and Canon amendments are almost never.

## The Legal Architecture

It exists, it's deliberate, and it lives with counsel — not in your daily work.

Underneath all of this is a formal, multi-jurisdiction legal and trust architecture that holds the entities, protects the mission from capture, and safeguards the capital. It is intentionally designed and carefully governed. **The specifics live with the Foundation and counsel**, not in day-to-day operations — so this handbook names that it exists without detailing it. If your work ever touches it, you'll be brought in directly.

## The Invitation

If the shape makes sense and the work feels like yours.

If you have read this and the work feels like yours — if you want to spend your working life building the infrastructure both edges will run on — we want you in it. Bring your full skill, your whole self, and your honest disagreement when it is warranted. That is the only requirement at the door.

Everything else — the tiers, the ministers, the entities — is just how that one sentence gets organized so the work can actually move.
