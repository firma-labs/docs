## 04 — ON FEAR `LOCKED`

Fear is not a value. Fear is not an ethos. Fear is not a principle. Fear is a posture this organization explicitly rejects — and we name it directly because fear that goes unnamed becomes the loudest voice in the room. Fear that is named loses its grip.

Doctrine, not commentary. Top-level by design.

### 04.1 — The Doctrine

> **Fear is not a sin; it is a signal.**
>
> The work asks you to receive the signal, name it honestly, and act anyway. The covenant holds whether or not the fear holds.
>
> We hire people who have learned to do this. We build a culture where doing it is normal. We protect the people who do it from being punished for it.

### 04.2 — What This Means In Practice

- **Fear is information, not authority.** You feel it. You name it. You proceed.
- **Fear-shaped decisions are the failure mode we care about.** Staying small, hitting safe targets, taking shots you knew you could make — these are the decisions a quiet fear produces. They look like prudence. They are not.
- **The honor value of "Boldness — willingness to say the true thing" is fear's antagonist at the level of speech.** Boldness is not the absence of fear. It is the decision to speak anyway.
- **"Radical faith in the impossible" is fear's antagonist at scale.** Faith is not naïveté about how hard the work is. It is the refusal to let fear of the work's size become the reason it doesn't get built.
- **Decisive Peace is fear's antagonist as tempo.** Paralysis is fear in slow motion. Rashness is fear in fast motion. Decisive Peace is what fear cannot produce.

### 04.3 — The Three Antagonists Of Fear

| Antagonist | Layer | Where It Lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Boldness** | Speech | Honor value — "willingness to say the true thing" |
| **Radical Faith** | Scale | Ethos #5 — "the only rational response to a mission this large" |
| **Decisive Peace** | Tempo | Foundational Principle #4 — "fast decisions from union, not hurry" |

These are not the same thing. We name all three because fear shows up at all three layers — what you say, what you attempt, and how fast you move when you do.

### 04.4 — Where This Shows Up Structurally

- **In hiring (§05)** — *Unintimidated* as the 7th hard criterion. We screen for it. We score green/red signals on it.
- **In growth (§09)** — fear-shaped careers (safe shots, plateau, coasting) are named as the failure mode, not a quiet preference.
- **In leaving (§14)** — nobody stays at Firma Labs out of fear. Going in peace is honored, including the leaving that comes from God saying go.
- **In Anti-Baal (§06.5)** — no clawback for speaking truth. Compensation does not become a leash, self-imposed or otherwise.

### 04.5 — The Cultural Discipline

We do not pretend fear isn't real. We pretend we don't notice it, and that pretense is what kills the work. **Naming fear is how Firma Labs stays a place where the true thing can still be said out loud.**

This is why §04 sits where it does — above hiring, above compensation, above growth. Not because fear is more important than those things, but because all of those things bend wrong if fear isn't named first.

## 05 — HIRING — WHO WE HIRE AND HOW `LOCKED`

### 05.1 — The Hiring Predicate (Handbook Language)

> **Powerful people with a moral framework.**

Not high performers. Not talent. Powerful — carries weight, moves things, doesn't wait for permission. Paired with a moral framework so load-bearing that we never have to ask where they are or what they're doing.

### 05.2 — The 7 Hard Criteria

Used in first-pass resume/application filtering and Stage 1 screen:

1. **Powerful** — they would still be doing the right work without direction
2. **Moral framework** — load-bearing, whatever it is
3. **Purpose over career arc** — compelled by what we're building, not optimizing for next title
4. **Willing to deploy** — Patagonia, Namibia, Kazakhstan excites them
5. **Agentic-fluent** — already uses agentic leverage, or hungry to learn
6. **Radical faith** — believes the impossible is often just the un-attempted
7. **Unintimidated** — they don't make decisions from fear. Not fear of failure, fear of looking foolish, fear of the room, fear of the unknown country, fear of the spiritual dimension of the work. Fear is information they receive but not authority they obey. Sits next to Radical Faith — faith is the affirmative posture, unintimidated is the negative one. **Internal-only criterion** — the handbook's six are the public version; this is what we score in interviews. See §04 for the underlying doctrine.

### 05.3 — What We Do Not Hire For (Handbook)

- Not resumes
- Not pedigree
- Not "culture fit" as it's usually meant
- **Character and capacity** — and we interview to understand both

Compensation flows through three layers. The structure is settled. The mechanics of layers 2 and 3 are being designed.

### 06.3 — Economic Rights `IN PROGRESS`

Economic Rights are the formal claim. Fragmints are the on-chain representation. The accrual mechanism, vesting curve, and treatment on departure are being designed. Until that work is complete, no Economic Rights are being formally issued and no Fragmints are being minted.

What is settled:
- Economic Rights are **permanent and irrevocable** once accrued — they are not equity, not options, not tokens, not subject to clawback for any reason except those defined in Canon (fraud, theft, betrayal of the Nation, intentional harm).
- Economic Rights survive departure — the claim does not end when the role ends.
- The on-chain representation lives on the FIRMA L2 (OP Stack) and is auditable.

What is `IN PROGRESS`:
- Accrual mechanics — how Economic Rights accumulate over time and against what work
- Valuation — how Economic Rights are priced relative to the underlying claim
- Distribution mechanics — how dividends or value flows reach the holder
- Tax and entity treatment across jurisdictions
- The relationship between the contribution accrual layer and the on-chain Fragmint representation

### 06.4 — Trust `IN PROGRESS`

The Trust layer provides long-term sovereign provision held in trust structure. The full mechanics — activation timing, vesting, departure scenarios, yield, humanitarian tithe percentage, beneficiary rules, jurisdictional structure (Cook Islands, Cayman, or other) — are actively being designed.

What is settled in principle:
- The Trust is structured around provision, not extraction — boring money is holy money
- A humanitarian tithe to CIK is anticipated to be hardcoded into the trust deed (percentage `IN PROGRESS`)
- Anti-Mammon and Anti-Baal provisions (§06.5) apply regardless of which mechanics are chosen

What is `IN PROGRESS`:
- Activation timing (Day 90, Year 2, or other)
- Vesting schedule (cliff, graded, immediate, or other)
- Yield mechanics (perpetual, term-limited, or other)
- Departure scenarios (yield continues, yield stops, conditional, or other)
- Beneficiary and succession rules
- Trust jurisdiction and structure
- Tithe percentage and recipient mechanics

This is the single largest open work-stream in People Ops. Until it is finalized, no Trust is being opened for any team member and no offer letter references the Trust as an active benefit.

### 06.5 — Anti-Mammon / Anti-Baal Provisions `LOCKED`

These principles apply across all three compensation layers and will be hardcoded into the Trust deed when finalized.

**Anti-Mammon:**
- Flat salary bands — no negotiation, no comparison, no envy
- No lump sum, no exit event, no IPO to chase
- Wealth is structurally outward-flowing
- On-chain transparency

**Anti-Baal:**
- **No clawback for speaking truth** — compensation cannot be revoked, reduced, or delayed for disagreeing with leadership, voting against a proposal, prophesying correction, or raising concerns
- **Covenant breach is defined in Canon, not by a manager's opinion** — fraud, theft, betrayal of the Nation, intentional harm. NOT disagreement, dissent, prophecy, or leaving.
- **No golden handcuffs** — leave when God says leave

## 08 — GROWTH

> **Class system under construction.** The class system that names the relationships people hold to the work and the network is being redesigned. The earlier four-class framework (Advisor / Fellow / Associate / Partner) is no longer in force, and a new class structure is `IN PROGRESS`. ### 08.1 — The Class System `IN PROGRESS`

The class system describes the relationships a person can hold to the work and the network — what they own, what authority they carry, how they are compensated, how they can move between modes. The earlier framework is no longer in force.

What is settled in principle:
- Classes describe **relationships to the work**, not corporate titles or rungs on a ladder
- Movement between classes is bidirectional and honored — a person stepping up is celebrated, a person stepping into a different mode is also celebrated
- Class is decoupled from compensation (§06.2 salary classes are operational bands, not the same as growth classes)

What is `IN PROGRESS`:
- The class names themselves
- The number of classes
- The definitions of each class — what it is, what it owns, what it carries, how it is held
- Movement criteria between classes
- Compensation alignment between class and salary band
- Onboarding and offboarding logic per class

When this work is finalized, the operational definitions live here and Section 08.3 (transition mechanics) is filled in.

### 08.2 — The Growth Principle `LOCKED`

The principle survives the class redesign. Whatever the classes end up being called, this is how growth works at Firma Labs.

> Growth at Firma Labs is a function of **trust extended** and **weight carried**, not time served.

- A person who steps up and carries more than their current class normally carries grows into the next class, full stop.
- A person who wants to step into a different mode — go build their own thing, return as something else — is honored, not punished.
- **"We would rather you fail at greatness than succeed at your own limits."** Growth trajectories are drawn for the hardest swing, not the safe landing.

**On the failure mode we actually care about:** The failure mode is fear-shaped — staying small, hitting a safe target, taking the shot you knew you could make, plateauing into competence. Fear that masquerades as prudence is still fear. The career we are happiest to promote is the one where the person admits they were scared, swung anyway, missed, recovered, and swung again bigger. The career we are slowest to promote is the one where every decision has visibly avoided risk. Trust extends to the person who takes the swing. (See §04 — On Fear.)

### 08.3 — Class Transitions `IN PROGRESS`

The mechanics of moving between classes — scoping the new role, compensation conversation, announcement — are pending the §08.1 redesign. Until then, transitions are handled 1:1 between $&#123;FOUNDER_CEO&#125;, $&#123;CPO&#125;, and the team member.

## 13 — HOW WE LEAVE `LOCKED` (in frame) · `IN PROGRESS` (in mechanics — see §06)

### 13.1 — The Frame (Handbook)

> Most companies treat departure as a failure mode. We treat it as the natural completion of a season.

### 13.2 — What Stays With You

- **Economic Rights** vest as accrued. Permanent, irrevocable. Pay value from everything you helped build for as long as the ecosystem runs. (Mechanics `IN PROGRESS` — see §06.3.)
- **Trust** — `IN PROGRESS`. The mechanics governing what happens to a Trust on departure are being designed (see §06.4).
- **Relationship with Firma Labs** — does not end. Come back to advise. Join another deployment. Step into a different mode.

### 13.3 — What We Ask of You

- Leave well
- Hand off with honor
- Tell the truth about why you're going
- Give feedback you wish someone had given six months earlier
- Close the loops you opened
- Bless the people coming up behind you

### 13.4 — Covenant Breach (Internal Only — Not Handbook)

Compensation is forfeited per the mechanics defined in the Trust deed when finalized. Covenant breach is defined in Canon, not management opinion:

- Fraud
- Theft
- Betrayal of the Nation
- Intentional harm

**NOT** disagreement, dissent, prophecy, speaking truth, voting against leadership, or leaving. These are protected under Anti-Baal.

### 13.5 — Death `IN PROGRESS`

Trust succession on death is part of the §06.4 mechanics design. The principle is that compensation passes to named beneficiaries; the operational detail (which beneficiaries are named, how succession is administered, what continues vs. what stops) is `IN PROGRESS`.

### 13.6 — Nobody Stays Out of Fear `LOCKED`

A covenant protection. Pairs with Anti-Baal (§06.5).

> **Nobody stays at Firma Labs out of fear.**
>
> Not fear of losing the trust. Not fear of leaving the mission. Not fear of what people will say. Not fear of admitting the season is over. Not fear of starting over somewhere else.
>
> If you're staying out of fear, you're already gone — go in peace.

Operationally, this means:

- **$&#123;CPO&#125; and $&#123;DISCERNMENT_LEAD&#125; actively check for it.** In Day 30 check-ins, in 90-day reviews, in the rare moment someone signals they're done but stays anyway. The question is asked plainly: *are you here because you want to be, or because you're afraid not to be?*
- **The trust does not become a leash.** Anti-Baal already protects against the trust being weaponized for compliance. This goes one step further — the trust does not become a *self-imposed* leash either. We tell people, plainly, that staying for the river when the work no longer fits is not what the river is for.
- **Leaving well is honored, including the leaving that comes from God saying go.** A person who hears a call elsewhere and follows it is not betraying the Nation. They are participating in the same Vine the Nation is rooted in.
- **The door stays open both ways.** The handbook already names that former team members are family and can return. The reverse is also true — if you're afraid to leave because you might want to come back, that fear is also not a reason to stay.

This is the operational counterpart to "no clawback for speaking truth." Anti-Baal protects the person who stays and prophesies. This protects the person who leaves in obedience to a different call.
